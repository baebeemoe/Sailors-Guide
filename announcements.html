OFFICIALUPDATE NOTICESEPTEMBER 2026

Star Sailors September Update Notice

GM Star Sailors · September 8, 2026

BEEMOE'S GUIDE SUMMARYSeptember roadmap and PC build delayed

The development team is undergoing internal adjustment while development efforts are refocused. During the delay, September operations include more frequent Alliance Raids, Partner rotations, daily login resources, known-issue fixes, and update-delay compensation.

01Update Delay02September Operation Plan03Known Issues04Apology Compensation
Read Official Announcement

✨ Our Voyage Continues ✨ Star Sailors!

Greetings, Adventurers!

First, we sincerely apologize for the concern caused by our recent lack of smooth communication and updates. We are carefully reviewing all the heartfelt criticism and concerns you have shared through the community. Today, we would like to apologize for the update delay and provide information on our September operation plan.

01

Update Delay

Star Sailors was created with the vision of reviving the classic JRPG turn-based combat system with a modern touch and charming characters. This vision still remains deeply rooted in our hearts.

To make this vision a reality, the <Star Sailors> development team is currently going through a period of transition and internal adjustment. We plan to refocus our development efforts to improve the game as a whole and get back on track with a better schedule.

To achieve this, some changes will be made to our development system, and we will need some time to complete this transition. As a result, the September update roadmap we had previously shared, as well as the PC build release, have unfortunately been delayed.

We sincerely apologize for disappointing Adventurers who have been waiting for the update content and PC build.

We understand that many Adventurers may be worried about future updates due to this sudden delay. We are currently communicating closely with the dev team to prevent any further schedule delays. Until then, the following temporary operation plan will be carried out.

We will prepare so that the delayed work and fixes can be applied to the live service sequentially as quickly as possible.

02

September Operation Plan

Including the Sep. 9 maintenance, the following events will be held first in September. We once again apologize that we are unable to immediately present the new roadmap we promised, and that we will be using existing content for the time being.

⚔ Alliance Raid Cycle ShortenedOnce per month → once every 2 weeks

To help ease the disappointment caused by the lack of new content, Alliance Raid will be held every 2 weeks.

  • Alliance Raid will begin on Sep. 16.
  • Rewards will change from the previous Gold Tier rewards to different rewards based on each tier.
✦ Pick Up and Trial Rotation

As the addition of new Partners has been delayed, Rotation Pick Up for existing Partners will open.

  • Caroline Rotation Pick Up and Trial opens on Sep. 9.
🎁 September Daily Login Rewards

Special login rewards will be distributed daily until September 23 to help with resource acquisition.

  • Bulging Mushroom ×2 — distributed twice daily: 7:00 PM–6:59 AM UTC and 7:00 AM–6:59 PM UTC.
  • Silver Compass ×2 + Golden Compass ×1 — distributed once daily.
03

Known Issues

We would like to inform you of in-game issues confirmed after the previous update. Some items are difficult to hotfix immediately due to the schedule adjustment, so we are first sharing the current status and will fix them sequentially as quickly as possible.

  1. Swimsuit Remi is not added to Normal Recruit after her Pick Up ended.

    Remi should be added to Normal Recruit after the Pick Up ends on Sep. 9, but this has not been applied. Remi is also not included in the random pool for Character Shards in the Exchange Center weekly shop.

  2. HP is fixed at 86% when equipping certain R-grade Artifacts.

    This appears to occur under certain conditions and with certain Artifacts. Reports are currently being checked.

  3. [View Clear Team] is available for the updated Rift, but not for previous chapters.

    When a new clear record is created, friends or guild members should be able to view it, but the record is currently missing from the list.

  4. The skill translation for UR Artifacts is incorrect in Simplified Chinese.

    The Resonance effect value is incorrectly displayed as [0.0n%]. This is only a display issue; as in other languages, the actual effect is applied correctly as +n.

04

Apology Compensation

As an apology and a token of gratitude to Adventurers who have been waiting, the following compensation has been prepared.

🎁 UPDATE DELAY APOLOGY COMPENSATION
Golden Compass ×10Diamond ×2,000Bulging Mushroom ×10
🗓 Compensation Distribution

From after the notice until Sep. 16 (Wed) 14:59 UTC.

Distribution Method: Sent once per server account to the Mailbox when logging in during the event period. Mail is stored for 7 days.

📍 Please Read

The delivered mail will be stored for 7 days, so please make sure to claim it before it expires.

We once again ask for your understanding regarding the disappointment caused by this schedule adjustment. When preparations are complete, we will return with a more stable and enjoyable <Star Sailors>. Once again, we sincerely apologize to all Adventurers who care for <Star Sailors>.
