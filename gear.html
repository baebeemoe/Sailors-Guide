Main Stat = Excluded Substat

A Gear piece cannot roll its current main stat again as a substat. Example: if a Necklace has Crit Rate as its main stat, Crit Rate is removed from that piece's possible substat pool.

RANDOM EFFECTS

Substats & Effect RNG

Gear substats are random. Each effect slot has an independent acquisition chance, while the Gear's main stat and already-acquired effects are excluded from later eligible substat calculations.

Effect Acquisition by Slot

First Rate Execution100%

About Grade-Specific Effect Rates

UC, R, SR and SSR each have their own effect value/rate tables. Possible effects include Additional ATK, Additional DEF, Additional HP, Crit Rate, Crit DMG, Heal Bonus, ACC, RES and Chain Mastery. Remember that a stat matching the Gear's current main stat is not available again as a substat.

BUILD BASICS

What Stats Should I Look For?

Always consider what the character's skills actually scale from.

DPS / ATTACKER

Damage Stats

Look for offensive stats such as ATK, Crit Rate and Crit DMG when they complement the character's kit.

DEFENDER

Match Their Scaling

Prioritize the defensive stat their abilities use. Some Defenders scale with DEF while others scale strongly with HP.

SUPPORTER

Kit-Dependent Utility

Healing, buffs and other support mechanics scale differently. Build around actual skill effects.

DEBUFFER

ACC Matters

ACC is especially important for Debuffers. More ACC improves their ability to successfully land debuffs.

BEEMOE'S TIP

Don't judge Gear only by rarity. A useful set effect, the right main stat, and strong compatible substats can matter more than simply equipping the highest-quality piece you own.
