ACQUISITION

Where to Get Gear

There are three useful places to obtain Gear or Gear-related resources.

⌖
PASSIVE ACQUISITION

Dispatch

Send units on Dispatch missions and collect the available rewards when the mission is complete.

HOME → BATTLE → DISPATCH
  1. Open Battle from Home.
  2. Select Dispatch.
  3. Choose an available Dispatch location.
  4. Assign units and collect rewards after completion.
⚔
GEAR FARMING

Conquest

Conquest is the dedicated battle mode for obtaining various Gear sets.

HOME → BATTLE → CONQUEST
  1. Open Battle.
  2. Select the Conquest · Gear tab.
  3. Choose an available encounter and clear it for Gear rewards.
◇
SHOP SOURCE

Exchange Center

The Exchange Center includes a Gear section containing Gear-related items and upgrade resources.

HOME → SHOP → EXCHANGE CENTER → GEAR
  1. Open Shop.
  2. Enter Mary's Exchange Center.
  3. Open Basic → Gear.

RANDOM EFFECTS

Substats & Effect RNG

Gear substats are random. Each effect slot has its own independent acquisition chance, and already-fixed or previously acquired effects are excluded from later calculations.

Effect Acquisition by Slot

First Rate Execution100%
Second Rate Execution50%
Third Rate Execution25%

Up to three effects can be acquired. The detailed effect rates change each time the process runs.

ACTUAL ACQUISITION RATEDisplayed Rate ÷ (100% − Total Rate of Excluded Effects)

Displayed decimal rates use up to four decimal places and are rounded at the fifth decimal place.

About Grade-Specific Effect Rates

UC, R, SR and SSR each use their own effect value/rate tables. The complete verified tables will be presented here in a compact searchable format rather than forcing players to read several long screenshots.

Possible effects include offensive, defensive and utility stats such as Additional ATK, Additional DEF, Additional HP, Crit Rate, Crit DMG, Heal Bonus, ACC, RES and Chain Mastery.

BUILD BASICS

What Stats Should I Look For?

There is no single stat rule for every unit. Always consider what the character's skills actually scale from.

DPS / ATTACKER

Damage Stats

Look for offensive stats such as ATK, Crit Rate and Crit DMG when they complement the character's kit.

DEFENDER

Match Their Scaling

Prioritize the defensive stat their abilities use. Some Defenders scale with DEF while others scale strongly with HP.

SUPPORTER

Kit-Dependent Utility

Healing, buffs and other support mechanics can scale differently. Build around the unit's actual skill effects rather than role name alone.

DEBUFFER

ACC Matters

ACC is especially important for Debuffers. More ACC improves their ability to successfully land debuffs, so it should be considered alongside any damage or utility needs.

BEEMOE'S TIP

Don't judge Gear only by its rarity. A useful set effect and strong substats that fit the character can matter more than simply equipping the highest-quality piece you own.
